# Partnership playbook

Portal `/v2` extends the existing institution catalog; old classes, consent attestations and reports are retained. New canonical workflow passports have institution type, country, website, language, timezone, coordinator, resources and independent verification state.

Stages reflect actual activity: found, interested, discussion, pilot_agreed, scheduled, started, completed, repeat or archived. Found contacts and logos are not partnerships. Starting an institution requires an actually started cohort. Refusal reasons are retained.

Prepare a limited pilot with audience, format, resources, budget/currency and criteria. Documents include proposal, program, responsibilities, memorandum, contract, pilot appendix and report. Fields cover parties/authority/goal/format/dates/resources/cost/data/publication/work rights/reschedule/termination/reporting/responsibility. They are drafts pending the parties' review, not automatically approved legal instruments.

Document workflow: draft → internal_review → institution_review → agreed → signed → active → completed/terminated. Signed requires a distinct authorized institution counterparty and confirmation source. Revised agreed terms preserve the old fields/signature evidence in a new history record and reset review/signature. A cohort cannot start against changed signed terms.

Representatives are nominated, independently confirmed in the institution scope, expire and can be revoked. A teacher is not automatically a verified institution representative. A cohort requires a confirmed agreement, responsible adults, program/dates/load/budget/criteria and consent. Enrollment is deduplicated in participant totals; real start and completion are explicit confirmations. Reports are current snapshots, not reconstructed historical months. Clubs record a responsible adult, resources and recurring rhythm.
